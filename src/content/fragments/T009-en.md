# Mehr Zeit

The road had [iced over](/fragments/F234/).  
I took a [slow train](/fragments/F027/).  
When I returned, you came forward to meet me,  
and together we left behind  
the strangers on the tram.  
You were already a history teacher:  
your eyes had become two little [lakes](/fragments/F107/).  
I peeled the onion on the water’s surface,  
layer by layer.  
Stinging tears flowed to the bottom of the lake.  
[Leaves](/fragments/F172/) like butterflies drifted in,  
while the winter duckshad not yet frozen.  
With a rush of wings,  
amid the praise of friends and fish,  
the tears ran onto the [railway](/fragments/T004/)     
tracksat the bottom of the lake.  
“Delayed again,”you said, puzzled.  
  
The ducks built a bridge across the water.  
We seemed to be drawing closer  
without ever having to wade in.  
It was happening in silence.  
  
“Mehr Zeit für dich,”  
read a [billboard](/fragments/F153/) at the Hauptbahnhof.  
[Time](/fragments/F166/) began to multiply.  
What kind of mind  
could grow leaves like these?  
  
Your mind wandered again.  
For a moment,  
you could somehow fall  
for two flowers at once.
