# Moth  
  
A moth flew into my [room](/fragments/F163/) and began crashing wildly into everything. One moment it darted toward a corner of the wall; the next it landed on the [printer](/fragments/A002/), then burrowed into [a pile of clutter](/fragments/F029/). When it struck my leg, a sudden panic ran through me.  And who among us hasn’t, for reasons we cannot explain, arrived in a bright and unfamiliar place, trying to make a life?  
  
Unfortunately, it had come to the wrong place. Poor little creature.  I trapped it beneath a glass jar and let it out through the [window](/fragments/V001/).  That was where it had come from, too.
