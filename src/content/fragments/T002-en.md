# The Strong-Willed Fish-Wanderer

A certain looseness  
among the rotting grass  
begins to unfurlits curled larval belly.  
A fish-wanderer roams—  
one that slipped from a [tank](/fragments/F156/)  
at the fish market,  
deathly afraid of the live,  
the raw, the living...  
At this moment,  
the [street](/fragments/F220/) is all war and turmoil.  
Within a moment spent playing with words,  
a [bud](/fragments/F169/) rests.  

[The white sun](/fragments/V015/) soaks in a [bucket](/fragments/F064/).  
Plop. Plop. Plop.  
The fish-wanderer moves along the roof ridge,  
out through a [white water pipe](/fragments/F160/)—  

and leaps into the sun.  
It passes through a black alley  
filled with blue-violet rooms.  
Rows of beautiful, strong-willed women  
stand in the doorways.  
The white fish-wanderer. 
springs up and down  
the grey steps.  

They—clamouring—  
are producing [lobsters](/fragments/F098/).
