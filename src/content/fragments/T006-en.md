# Breakthrough out of Malatang

Growing up, up, up.  
Never has a spring been this loud.  
  
From the [loudspeaker](/fragments/A040/) comes  
a smoke-stained repetition-  
childhood memory, repeated and repeated. 
  
[Brown-tinted glass](/fragments/F265/) slides back and forth  
in an old metal window frames,  
searching for a secret room--  
one that opens onto an agitated [sea](/fragments/F252/).  
  
Fiercely, we carry out our little experiments.  
as if we could devour the world  
and the grasslands.  
  
Can I make you fly?  
--beautiful elephant.  
  
Sentences hard to swallow,  
rice noodles hot against the tongue.  
Now I turn this place into an exit.  
  
Animals often wake at nights.  
  
“Walk straight ahead.  
Don’t sway from side to side,  
don’t stumble or stagger,”  
says the wolf.  
  
Dazed, I wake. 
and follow them forward.  
  
A crowd holding [blank albums](/fragments/F267/).  
moves in the opposite direction.  
  
Little bumps grow across my face.  
I feel them,  
from forehead to hairline-  
  
like a boxer  
whose punching bag is gone,  
my fingers stop  
in the smooth air.  
  
One punch...  
then another...  
  
Traces--  
intense traces that have been covered over...  
  
They make one feel pure.  
  
The raised bumps.   
along both sides of the neck...  
all the way to the roots of the hair.  
  
Hollowed out,  
I lie on the electric blanket.  
  
Flat beneath me, it scorches  
my back and hind legs.  
  
The wide and the narrow  
squeeze together at the edge of the bed.  
[Vapour](/fragments/F023/) retreats into its hole.  
  
The quilt climbs past my neck.  
The sheet is overgrown with the holllows.  
  
I lie there.  
  
In the corner of the wall,  
a [dewdrop](/fragments/F183/) like a lover  
hangs,  
forever peering out.
