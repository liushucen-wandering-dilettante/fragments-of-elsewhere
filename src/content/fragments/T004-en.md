# Typhoon

You are a door opening into distance  
When I cannot say it, cannot make it clear,  
I write down your image.  

“Why- not say it clearly?”  

Again and again,  
I am young man, with no craft to speak of,  
relying only on faint [threads of rain](/fragments/A021/)   
tapping against an egret  
in the sleepless nights.  

Two years later, I return by [boat](/fragments/F002/)  
 to the [rainy season in the south](/fragments/V005/) .  
Between dislocated sleep  
and latitude, and longitude,  
there stands a door.  

A woman on the train asks:  

“Why does the train across from us seem  
now near, now far?”  

Her ten-year-old son replies:  

“That’s just what [railways](/fragments/F228/) are like.”  

You have left again  
Of course, this is a good thing--  
a sign  
of your next arrival.
