# Guanyin Bodhisattva Says

On the [grassland](/fragments/T006/),  
a monk planted three sticks of incense  
for a lost sheep.  
  
I followed the ashes of the burning incense  
into a [pond](/fragments/F208/)  at summer’s end  
and found the laptop bag you had left behind,  
its zipper broken.  
  
Rain fell  
on that stick of [incense](/fragments/F054/).    
The sheep asked,    
“How many times must I bow down  
before I can find the way back  
to the sheepfold?”  
  
“It takes rain and flowers like these  
to provide for a pond like this,”  
said [Guanyin Bodhisattva](/fragments/F031/).  
  
At your bottom  
you hide a world,  
determined  
to keep it from my sight.   
  
Later, the sheep returned to its fold,  
but your pond had disappeared,  
  
playing hide-and-seek with me  
all over again.
