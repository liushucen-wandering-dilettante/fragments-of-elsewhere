# Four Seasons
  
Wandering through [the blank](/fragments/F181/)—  
snow-static, beer, [street-food stalls](/fragments/F082/).  
  
Food and work crowd in,  
crowding us out:  
me, and your imagination.  
  
Reading lines written by others,  
I imagine you,  
and your imagining—  
imagine what you imagine.  
  
Words cling to [your hand](/fragments/V016),  
busy clicking the mouse,  
painting with words.  
  
“Where have you gone off to this time?  
How are you busy through all four seasons?  
Not a single call all week.”  
  
“Being an artist means always being busy, Mom.”  
  
[A conversation](/fragments/A042/) I hear through the screen.  
  
Now I am outside the screen,  
living the life inside it.  
  
The explosives in the fish’s belly   
are drowned by the stream,  
gone mute.  
  
The faltering fish-mouth spits out [foam](/fragments/F284/).  
  
“So—you’re here.”  
  
I wake to check   
whether we are in the communal dormitory.  
  
Memory has wandered off again.  
  
At the Waking of Insects,  
a magnolia bud.  
speaks to the [mirror](/fragments/F267/),  
reflected behind the lake.  
  
Last year, I gathered    
a pocketful of word-marbles.  
  
Flick—  
off they fly toward someone’s face,  
  
clinging to your fingers,  
fly, fly—  
  
the music app,  
pause pressed.  
  
At last, at last,  
we are quiet.  
  
We escape into your tiny words:  
  
[I love you](/fragments/A031/).
