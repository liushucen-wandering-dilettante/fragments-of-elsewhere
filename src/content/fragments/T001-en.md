# Cat’s Tongue

Touch and hearing grow acute.  
partly because of the air,  
because of the frequency of vocal cord,  

because now it takes another tongue.  
to drink water.  

Have you been by that lake?  
Have you been in that lake?  
Have you been to that [lake](/fragments/F107/)...  

“I have. When there was still water.”  

One day, in a dream, she discovered  
that the cat in her dream had gone missing.  

“Is the cat by the lake swimming?”  
Drool runs down a [lotus leaf](/fragments/F109/).  
“The cat by the lake is [swimming](/fragments/T018/).”  

“Why show me a photograph of the lake?”  

Brewing hot tea in the [an air-conditioned room](/fragments/F050/),  
the evening [thunderstorm](/fragments/F179/) 
starts firing its [cannons](/fragments/A032/).    
Perhaps this is the distance between  
a lover and a sickle,  
or picking the crust from your eyes  
with your fingers in the morning.  

The cat yowls strangely,  
pacing back and forth,   
making friends with little dogs.  

Stay. Stay here.  
Keep away, Just keep away.
