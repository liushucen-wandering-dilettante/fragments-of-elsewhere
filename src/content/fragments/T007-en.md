# Her Fish

In this woman’s eyes  
lives an injured [fish](/fragments/F145/).  
I want to cup her in my hands  
and place her in a [river](/fragments/F167/)nearby,  
but she persists on remaining  
in this dampness.  
  
I walk into her.  
  
The lines of her lips   
are as lush as [leaves](/fragments/F187/).  
Her faces unfolds   
like a green plain.  
  
She says she wants to hold   
more fish and more trees.  
  
Gradually, her body  
recedes into a valley.  
The river passes through her mouths.  
and enters her stomach.  
  
The [water](/fragments/F210/) no longer churns.  
  
I walk from inside her.  
[Mist](/fragments/F184/)gathers on my face  
   
In my hands,  
I hold her fish.
