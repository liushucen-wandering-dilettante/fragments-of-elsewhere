# At Changsha Railway Station

I hadn’t been back to China for two years. Today, I took the train from Changsha to Shanghai.
Mum slipped two [lotus seed pods](/fragments/F250) into my bag. In the evening, I went to Dad’s place for [dinner](/fragments/F066/). He sent me away with ten salted duck eggs, ten tea eggs, three bottles of water, and the leftover chicken stewed with wood-ear mushrooms, packed into a lunchbox.  
  
Carrying two bags of food, I was seen off [at the entrance to the railway station](fragments/F093/) like a freshman leaving home for university.  
  
I passed [bare-chested men and people lying sprawled across the square and the corridors](fragments/F288/).  
  
Even at night, it was still hot.
