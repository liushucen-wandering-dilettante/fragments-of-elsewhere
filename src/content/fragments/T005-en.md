# Broadcast

Spring Equinox, [Mount Yuelu](/fragments/F269/)  
middle-aged people out on dates,  
college students are felling in love  
  
A red coffin   
is carried out  
through the back door of [the Fourth Municipal Hospital](/fragments/A024/)  
  
someone is carried away I am here  
funeral home, minivan, the [road](/fragments/F266/)   right on time  
a knock at the door a patient asks:  
“Can a fall of [rain](/fragments/F257/)--or an exam--still go on?”  
but the rain stops short,  
the many answers crumpled into a scarf--  
or a red blindfold  
walking from Qingming to Lixia  
  
Again on the [Juzizhou Bridge](/fragments/F214/)   
a doctor jumped  
there used to be signatures there  
written in gel pen  
  
Every [stall](/fragments/F082/) has a loudspeaker  
repeating what is for sale  
at the crossroads, [broadcasts](/fragments/A003/)  
from five directions  
a constant background  
  
in this smooth society  
everyone opens their mouth, strains their voice  
with amplifiers, loudspeakers  
hawking--if  
reluctantly to become  
a quiet nail  
or: self-made, self-sold  
reply: reply until replying over  
  
“Do you have experience creating viral content? ”  
HR asks me  
the shapeless, free world and I   
an comic and sorrowful slide  
I become a working person——or work itself  
on the homepage  
more can be done  
  
I feel my fingers, toes  
the body is here  
the finishing unfished  
  
Moldy days hang overhead  
I peel away  
layers after layers of self-description  
and become a smooth ball  
  
In the [river](/fragments/F002/) I scoop up  
a hand of blue waterweed  
with a slurp  
it slips away
